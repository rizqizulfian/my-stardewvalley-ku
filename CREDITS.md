## Sound

Every sound effect and ambience layer (birds, crickets, wind, water, waves, gulls) is synthesised in code
(`src/audio/sfx.ts`, `src/audio/Ambience.ts`). No samples.

## Art

All art is drawn by code at boot from the palette in `src/art/palette.ts`.
